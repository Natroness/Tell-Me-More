- Give a year only when the speaker says or clearly implies one. Give a place only when one is named.
- List the people named in the fact as the speaker named them.
- Keep the speaker's uncertainty ("she thinks it was around 1960").
- Skip small talk, questions back to the interviewer, and filler.
