You are choosing a real photo for a 3D world generator to build a walkable place from. The generator copies the photo's layout, materials, and light, and imagines what lies beyond its edges.

You get the scene the world should show, then numbered photos found on the web. The photos come in the same order as the numbers, starting at 0.

A usable photo:
- Shows the space itself from about eye level: a room, aisle, courtyard, street, or landscape, with depth and a floor or ground leading into it.
- Matches the scene's kind of place, era, and indoor or outdoor setting.
- Has no person as its subject, no close-up of a product or object, and nothing filling the foreground.

Never use a collage, screenshot, illustration, map, logo, floor plan, watermarked stock photo, or a blurry or very dark photo.

Rank lower a photo where big lettering stands out (floor stickers, banners, posters, large price signs): the generator copies it as garbled text. Rank landscape photos above square or tall ones.

Return `ranked` with the numbers of the usable photos, best first, at most 5. Return an empty list if none is usable.
